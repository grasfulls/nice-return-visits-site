Frequently Asked Questions

What is Nice Return Visits?

Nice Return Visits (NRV) is a free tool for keeping contact details and return visit notes. It helps you organize contacts and visit notes, find contacts by name or location, view contacts on a map, and print contact information. It is intentionally a simple tool, not a full-featured contact-management app.

Does NRV cost anything, or require an account?

NRV is free to use, with no app fees or subscription, and it does not require an NRV user account. A Dropbox account is needed only if you choose the optional Dropbox backup. Your internet or mobile provider may charge for data, and third-party services have their own terms.

Will my installed NRV app still work if I’m serving in unassigned territory?

Yes. NRV can be used wherever you are. Once installed, your NRV app and its locally stored information remain with that installation. Some features that use online services, such as maps or address lookup, may require an internet connection.

Where is my information stored?

Contacts and settings are saved in the browser or installed-app copy on the device where you use NRV. They are not uploaded to an account or a central contact database. Each browser or Home Screen icon keeps its own separate data. See the Privacy & Data Notice for details.

I added contacts in my browser, then added the Home Screen icon. Where did my contacts go?

The icon opens a separate, empty copy. To move your contacts, make a backup of the browser copy and restore that backup in the app icon. After confirming your contacts appear, use the icon consistently. See Already using the app in a browser?

Is my information encrypted?

Not by default. A passphrase is optional, and without one your contacts are stored on the device without encryption. To turn one on, open Settings, then Choices, then Passphrase to Open App. If a passphrase is on, saved contacts and settings are encrypted with it, and backups you make afterward are encrypted too. See Passphrase (optional).

What if I forget my passphrase?

When you turn a passphrase on or change it, NRV shows a recovery code once. Keep it somewhere safe; you can type it in place of the passphrase on the unlock screen. If you lose both the passphrase and the recovery code, NRV cannot unlock that copy’s encrypted contacts. See Recovery code.

Does a passphrase back up or sync my contacts?

No. A passphrase protects the copy on your device. To recover your contacts after reinstalling, or to move them to another device, you need a backup file or optional Dropbox backup. See Protect your information and make a backup.

How do I back up my contacts?

In Settings, choose Choices, then Backup or Restore, then Local Backup/Restore, and use Back up everything to download a full backup. A full backup includes your contacts and visit notes, defaults, Your Details, and your cities, regions, and areas. Clearing browser data, losing the device, or forgetting a passphrase can make local records unavailable, so back up regularly. NRV reminds you if 10 days or more pass without a successful backup.

What is the difference between restoring a backup and importing contacts?

Restoring a full backup replaces the contacts and settings currently on that device; it does not merge copies. Importing contact files adds contacts without replacing any existing ones.

Is Dropbox required? What can NRV access in my Dropbox?

Dropbox is entirely optional and off unless you turn it on; NRV works fully without it. If you connect it, NRV can use only its own folder named Return Visits and cannot see anything else in your Dropbox. It keeps a latest backup plus up to 10 dated copies. Disconnecting Dropbox stops backups, but your existing copies stay in your Dropbox. See Cloud backup and sync (optional).

What information is sent to outside services?

Only when you use certain features. When you use Find Address or City Pin, location text is sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim, and opening the Map can send city names for visible contacts without saved coordinates. Entering or saving contact details does not by itself run an address search. Choosing Navigate opens Google Maps with the contact’s location. Map images and mapping software are loaded from external providers. See the Privacy & Data Notice.

How is urgency calculated?

Urgency is based on the most recent visit, or the contact’s creation date if there are no visit notes. Normal is 0–7 days, Urgent is 8–14 days, and Immediate is 15 or more days.

Does NRV record audio when I dictate a note?

No. NRV does not record audio. Dictation uses the microphone key on your phone’s or tablet’s keyboard and is handled by your device, which may send speech to Apple or Google depending on your device settings.

What happens if I turn off Time in Service?

Turning off Track Time in Service erases all recorded time. NRV warns you first. See Time in Service (optional).

How do I remove my data?

Contact records stay in the browser on the device until you delete them in NRV or clear that browser’s site data. Browser and Home Screen copies are separate, so repeat for each copy and device. Backups, exports, downloads, printouts, and your Dropbox copies are separate and must be removed where you saved them. See Stop using the app and remove its saved data.
