Keep information for your “Nice” return visits organized and close at hand.

NRV is a free, installable web app that helps you keep contact information, visit history, notes, and follow-up information organized in one place.

NRV

Core Functions

Everything you need for your return visits

Keep the everyday information and tools for your return visits together in one place.

Contacts & Visit History

Keep contact information, notes, and visit history together.

Find & Organize

Search, filter, and sort contacts, and organize them using Cities, Regions, and Areas.

Maps, Locations & Directions

View contacts on a map, use address and location tools, place or adjust pins, and get directions to your return visits.

Print & Share

Print or share the information you need.

Backup & Restore

Create backups of your NRV information and restore them when needed.
