Privacy & Data Notice

Last updated November 5, 2026

Download this notice as a PDF.

At a glance

Before first use on a browser or installed-app copy, NRV asks you to read and agree to the Terms of Use and Privacy & Data Notice. Agreeing allows the next step. It does not connect Dropbox, send a contact, or run an address search.

For regular phone use, I recommend adding NRV to your Home Screen; it opens like an app while keeping contacts locally on your device. The browser and Home Screen versions may store separate data and do not automatically sync unless optional Dropbox cloud backup is connected in each copy. Adding the icon does not transfer contacts already saved in your browser. To switch using a backup file, export a backup (encrypted or not, your choice) from the version with your data and restore it in the other version – See the Help page.

NRV keeps your contact records in browser storage on the device where you use NRV. NRV does not require an NRV user account or use a central contact database. Dropbox is optional and must be chosen separately; to enable backup and sync, connect and authorize your Dropbox account in each device or app copy. A passphrase is optional; if you turn one on in Settings, NRV encrypts saved contacts and settings with it. Without a passphrase, records are stored unencrypted in your browser and, if you choose Dropbox, in your Dropbox backups. Your records do not automatically sync to another device unless Dropbox is connected and authorized in both copies. I do not maintain a list identifying who downloads or uses NRV.

Some features connect to outside services. When you choose Find Address or City Pin, location text can be sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Searches may include street address, City, State/Province, Country, and a cross-street or intersection when you choose that option. When the Map opens, visible contacts without saved coordinates can cause each distinct City, State/Province, and Country to be sent to Nominatim to place approximate city pins.

If no contact, home, or live-location pin is available, the Default City, State/Province, and Country may also be sent to Nominatim to center the map approximately. Entering or saving contact details does not by itself run the contact-address search. Find from address in Settings → Defaults & Your Details sends that address to Nominatim. With permission, the Map uses device location while it is open to show your live position as a blue dot. NRV does not record audio; keyboard dictation is handled by your device. Map images and mapping software are loaded from external providers.

Information stored on your device

NRV stores contact and visit information you enter, including names, email addresses, phone numbers, Country, State/Province, City, Region, Area, street address, map coordinates, and visit notes. It also stores app settings and Time in Service data when that optional feature is enabled. Saved records are stored in browser IndexedDB storage. If you turn on a passphrase, saved contacts and settings are encrypted using a key derived from it; otherwise they are stored unencrypted, and anyone with access to the device and browser profile may be able to read them. NRV decrypts protected records in the browser while you use them.

Each browser or installed-app copy keeps its own local data. Data is sent to Dropbox only if you choose to connect optional Dropbox cloud backup. When connected, NRV can upload backups, check for newer Dropbox data, download compatible backup data, combine supported local and Dropbox changes in the browser, and upload the combined result. NRV code does not create an application user account or central contact database.

You can turn on, change, or turn off the passphrase for the current app copy in Settings (entering the existing passphrase when changing or turning off one). NRV re-encrypts (or decrypts) that copy's saved contacts and settings locally. This local operation does not itself change other app copies or existing backup files. Existing encrypted backups continue to require the passphrase they were created with; existing unencrypted backups remain unencrypted.

Information sent to other services

  • Address search: If you choose Find Address or City Pin, NRV sends location search text to OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Depending on what you entered, this can include street address, City, State/Province, and Country. It may also search the street name without its leading house number. Search Near Cross-Street sends the intersection with the selected location. When the Map opens, visible contacts without saved coordinates can cause distinct City, State/Province, and Country combinations to be sent to Nominatim for approximate city pins. If there are no contact, home, or live-location pins, the Default City, State/Province, and Country may be sent to Nominatim to center the map approximately.

    Entering or saving contact details does not by itself run the contact-address search. Opening the Map can run the city lookups described above. Do not use these lookup features or the Map if you do not want the relevant location information sent to Nominatim.

  • Navigation: If you choose Navigate on a contact card or map pin, NRV opens Google Maps directions using the contact's saved coordinates or address. That destination is sent to Google Maps. Do not use the navigation link if you do not want that location information sent to Google.
  • Contact sharing: If you choose Share and select Text / SMS, WhatsApp, or Email, NRV opens that service with a plain-text summary of the contact's name and available phone, email, address, and directions link. Visit notes are included only if you choose to include the most recent note or all notes in the Share dialog; the default is none. The selected service receives the details when its composer opens, and its privacy practices apply to those details. They are sent to another person only if you complete sending. This plain-text sharing option is not encrypted by NRV; use the contact export for app-to-app transfers.
  • Map display: When you open or use a map, your browser requests map tiles from OpenStreetMap tile servers. Those requests reveal the map areas being viewed and may reveal ordinary connection information, such as your device's IP address, to the tile provider.
  • Mapping software: The Leaflet mapping library is loaded from unpkg.com. Your browser connects to that provider to retrieve the library used to display the map.
  • Hosting: NRV files are delivered from GitHub Pages. Like other web hosting providers, the provider may process technical request information when your browser retrieves the site. Consult the provider's current privacy information for details.
  • Device location: If you choose Use Device Location and grant permission, the browser asks the device for its current location and can use the returned coordinates to position a contact or home pin. When you open the Map and grant permission, NRV can also watch device location while the Map is open and show current position as a blue dot with an accuracy area. Location accuracy can vary.
  • Dropbox (optional): Only if you connect Dropbox in Settings, your browser contacts Dropbox to sign in and uses the NRV folder in your Dropbox account. It can upload backups, check for and download newer copies, combine compatible local and cloud changes in the browser, and upload the combined result. If the local copy and the Dropbox backup use different passphrases, automatic combining can be prevented and automatic backup can pause until you decide whether to restore the Dropbox backup locally or replace it with a backup of the local data.

NRV does not include analytics, advertising, registration, or an in-app user-tracking feature. I do not collect or maintain information identifying who downloads, visits, or uses NRV, or how they use it. Because users are not required to register or sign in and I do not receive user-identifying or usage reports, I cannot verify an end-user's identity, location, age, eligibility, or intended use of NRV. This describes information I collect through NRV; it does not mean that no technical request records exist. GitHub Pages and third-party providers may process connection or request information under their own practices, as described above. I do not control those systems or necessarily have access to their logs. Review the providers' current terms and privacy notices for more detail.

Backups, exports, printing, and sharing

  • Local backup files and contact exports are created only when you choose the relevant export action. NRV downloads a file (encrypted when a passphrase is on; unencrypted otherwise). To enable optional Dropbox cloud backup and sync, connect your Dropbox account in NRV’s Settings and authorize NRV to use its Return Visits folder in your Dropbox account. Simply having or using Dropbox does not enable this feature. After you connect and authorize it, and provided automatic backup is not paused by a passphrase mismatch, NRV automatically uploads the latest backup about 15 seconds after a saved change and tries to upload any pending backup when you leave or hide the app. When you open NRV, it also backs up if the last Dropbox backup was at least a day ago, subject to the same pause condition. Backup and syncing cannot continue while NRV is completely closed; each device or app copy needs its own Dropbox connection.
  • Dropbox cloud backup (optional, off by default): if you connect your Dropbox account in Settings, NRV uploads full backups to its app folder in Dropbox, keeping a latest copy and up to 10 dated daily copies. Each backup is encrypted with the NRV passphrase in use when it is created, if one is on; otherwise it is not encrypted. Compatible newer Dropbox data can be downloaded and merged locally with this device before a combined copy is uploaded. NRV stores a Dropbox access token in the browser so the connection can continue. Disconnecting removes the access token and asks Dropbox to revoke it. Dropbox's own terms and privacy policy apply to files in your Dropbox account. I do not receive your Dropbox credentials or your backups.
  • You choose where to save, store, or share exported files. Anyone who obtains an unencrypted backup can read it. Anyone who obtains an encrypted file would also need its passphrase to decrypt it, so keep the passphrase separate from the file.
  • Restoring a full backup replaces the local contacts and settings on the device after confirmation.
  • If you print or save a printout as a PDF, contact information is sent to the printer or PDF destination you select through your device or browser. Protect printed pages and saved PDFs as you would other contact records.

Your choices and responsibilities

  • You can choose whether to use address lookup, device location, maps, exports, and printing. Map tiles and address lookup require an internet connection.
  • If you turn on a passphrase, keep it and your recovery code private. Make backups regularly. NRV cannot recover a forgotten passphrase unless you still have the recovery code.
  • If you clear browser storage, remove NRV, lose the device, or forget a passphrase you turned on without a usable backup, your local records may become unavailable.
  • You are responsible for entering contact information appropriately and for deciding whether you have permission to store, export, print, or share it.

Retention and deletion

Contact records remain in the browser on the device until you delete them in NRV or clear that browser's site data. Browser and Home Screen app copies may store data separately, so removing one copy may not remove another. On iPhone or iPad, clear the Safari browser copy in iOS Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data; remove a separate Home Screen app copy by touching and holding its icon, choosing Remove App, then Delete App. In Chrome on Android, use the site's Clear data or Clear & reset setting, or go to Settings → Site settings → All sites. Menu names may vary by version. Repeat on every browser and device used. Clearing local site data is separate from deleting the backups stored in your Dropbox account. Exported backup files, contact files, downloads, printed pages, and PDFs are also separate copies; clearing site data does not delete them. Remove those separately wherever you saved or shared them.

Changes and what I can know

I do not collect user-identifying information or receive usage reports, so I cannot identify individual users or verify who they are or any details about them. I update this notice if NRV's storage, network connections, providers, or features change. Technical request information that hosting or mapping providers may process is described above; I do not control those systems or necessarily have access to their records.
