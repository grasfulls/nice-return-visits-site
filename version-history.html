What's New

0.9.53 — More room for your contacts

  • NRV uses the top of the screen more efficiently, giving your contacts more room.

0.9.52 — Keep your information when installing

  • Use NRV in your browser, or add its icon for regular use.
  • Back up existing NRV information before moving it to an installed copy; the original is not erased.

0.9.51 — Installation improvements

  • NRV can recognize many devices and browsers and show steps for adding its icon. You can correct the selection if needed.

0.9.5 — More ways to organize and plan

  • Choose interface language and Country Addressing independently, including per-contact countries.
  • Organize Working Need Greater contacts, focus the map, filter results, and export only the contacts shown.
  • Create personal ministry goals, choose methods and milestones, track progress, and preserve achievements.

0.9.0 — Map and update tools

  • Choose a saved city or add one while editing a contact.
  • Place and adjust contact map pins, with cross-street search when needed.
  • See your current NRV version and check for updates in Settings.
