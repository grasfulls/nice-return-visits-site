What's New

0.9.065 — Help for startup and setup errors

  • Select an error code to see what it means, how long to wait before retrying, and when to ask for help.

0.9.064 — Clearer Peru address terminology

  • The Peru address field is now labeled Territory.

0.9.063 — Clearer goals and safer corrections

  • Edit an in-progress goal and undo an accidental step completion without losing other progress.
  • Goal setup explains manual progress and optional time goals, with clearer completed-step counts.
  • Field explanations appear before their controls, and safe recovery screens offer OK.

0.9.062 — Safer startup and data protection

  • NRV verifies the page, app, service worker, and build metadata before opening saved information.
  • No-passphrase data opens normally; incompatible data stays unchanged behind a safe update screen.

0.9.058 — Optional features organized in Settings

  • Time in Service, Goals & Milestones, Encouragement Messages, and Working Need Greater are now grouped under Settings → Options.
  • Open Goals & Milestones from Options; its page no longer shows Contacts search and filters.

0.9.57 — Country-scoped locations and reliable updates

  • Each country now keeps its own independent Cities, Regions, and Areas lists, so one country's entries are never offered for another country's contacts.
  • The app update prompt now checks the actual build and no longer repeats after you update.

0.9.56 — Independent cities, regions, and areas

  • Regions and areas are now saved once as independent reusable lists, with no city required, and any contact can use any saved city, region, and area together.

0.9.55 — A clearer path from installation to NRV

  • Choose or correct your device and browser before installing, then follow the matching steps and open the new NRV icon to continue setup.

0.9.54 — A smoother installation experience

  • Install NRV with a clearer device-specific guide, a safe handoff to the new icon, and a cleaner Contacts toolbar.

0.9.52 — Keep your information when installing

  • Use NRV in your browser, or add its icon for regular use.
  • Back up existing NRV information before moving it to an installed copy; the original is not erased.

0.9.51 — Installation improvements

  • NRV can recognize many devices and browsers and show steps for adding its icon. You can correct the selection if needed.

0.9.5 — More ways to organize and plan

  • Choose interface language and Country Addressing independently, including per-contact countries.
  • Organize Working Need Greater contacts, focus the map, filter results, and export only the contacts shown.
  • Create personal ministry goals, choose methods and milestones, track progress, and preserve achievements.

0.9.0 — Map and update tools

  • Choose a saved city or add one while editing a contact.
  • Place and adjust contact map pins, with cross-street search when needed.
  • See your current NRV version and check for updates in Settings.
